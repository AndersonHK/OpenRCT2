## Findings

The construction strips show the ghost elevated path decks, slopes and railings, the yellow construction arrow, and the selection outlines in all four rotations at zooms 0 and 1. There is no wholesale disappearance of these overlays in this saved fixture. The reference support posts are missing from native output. The raised selected water patch also lacks its vertical sides; the native result appears as a flat water sheet above exposed grass/selection outlines. Root has a later candidate change for the water sides, but these images still fail and remain the evidence for 114.

An additional concrete discrepancy is the exposed hillside path portal, visible at rotations 1 and 2 at both zooms. The upstream portal is an open dark arch; native output has a grey filled/door-like face and a bottom ledge. The enlarged, unfiltered pixel crop `obj/vulkan-parity/construction-track114-manual-audit/portal-r1-z0-detail.png` preserves reference/native/difference panels. This looks like an incorrect portal component or frame/composition, not an absent ghost path. The image alone does not establish whether selection, door state, or ordering causes it.

The main authored rail geometry is visible in every one of the 49 track specimens across all eight views, including the multi-sequence, chain, inverted and steep/vertical pieces. No wholly missing rail piece was identified in this finite corpus. This is a visual presence finding, not exact pixel parity. Support columns, station underframes, and large support trestles remain visibly absent; terrain/background differences also contribute to the exact differences.

Station specimens 12 (style 3, ride type 8) and 13 (style 69, ride type 78) have a separate horizontal deck discrepancy. In the full-size rotation-0/zoom-0 strips the reference has a brown platform/deck under the side fences; native output exposes a grass gap beneath otherwise present fences and track. This should be tracked separately from the missing vertical support columns. A future comparison must establish whether the omitted deck is generated by a support recipe or station-body recipe; a common ordering change cannot be assumed to restore absent art.

Additional full-size checks of specimen 21 at rotation 1/zoom 0 and specimen 46 at rotation 3/zoom 0 confirmed that their running rail/surface is present; the large differences there predominantly depict missing support structures. The reviewed sheets did not establish another missing main track shape. Fine join, palette, clipping and overlap differences have not been waived.

The grouped sheets are a macro-geometry review and cannot substitute for pixel-exact comparison.
