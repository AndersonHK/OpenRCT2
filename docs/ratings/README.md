# Ride ratings and experience sampling

The [aggregate rationale](ride-rating-aggregate-rationale.md) owns the scoring model; the [stat-source manifest](ride-rating-stat-source-manifest.md) traces its inputs. The local-context page explains spatial sampling and invalidation. Keep tuning values in these owners and source, not in duplicated status summaries. The [kart calibration archive](../archive/ratings/README.md) retains synthetic comparisons, selected follow-up and rejected alternatives; those reports do not establish general gameplay balance.
